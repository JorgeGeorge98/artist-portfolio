# Gallery wall material

Asset: `public/textures/gallery-quartz.webp` (1254 × 1254 pixels, 310,432 bytes).

Created with the built-in image-generation tool and converted to WebP at quality 91 without resizing. The original generated PNG is retained in the generation library. This texture is decorative architecture and is not attributed to Daniela Salem.

The renderer uses it as an sRGB base-color map and a separate linear-data roughness map, plus fine procedural bump grain. Slab-scale texture repetition, nonmetallic satin response, and a restrained clearcoat let the gallery lights reveal the surface. The ceiling remains matte, and the floor has finer grain. All maps are loaded before the gallery loading state clears and disposed when it closes.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless physically based base-color texture for the walls of a minimalist fine-art gallery in a Three.js scene, not a rendered room. Create a high-definition square 2048x2048 seamless tileable flat material swatch of warm ivory honed quartz-look architectural porcelain. Top-down orthographic macro surface scan, evenly lit diffuse albedo only, no directional light, no shadows, no reflections baked into the image. Pale warm ivory mineral base, fine dense crystalline speckles with softly mottled cloudy mineral variation and several delicate naturally meandering warm-grey and faint taupe veins. The texture must be clearly tactile and convincingly real stone while restrained enough behind colorful paintings. A sophisticated large-format contemporary gallery surface: satin honed, small pores, varied fine mineral aggregate, occasional tiny sand-colored inclusions. Veins are sparse, thin, soft and organic, not dramatic black marble, not gold metallic, not cracks. All four edges must match seamlessly when repeated. Entire image is the flat stone texture, edge to edge; no tile borders, no grout, no slab edge, no perspective, no objects, no typography, no logo, no frame, no transparency.
